Description

Loctite Adhesives, Sealants & Threadlockers represent the world’s most comprehensive line of engineered chemical solutions for industrial assembly, maintenance, and repair applications. As a Henkel brand with over 60 years of innovation, Loctite has become synonymous with reliability in preventing fastener loosening, creating leak-proof seals, and forming permanent bonds across virtually every industry worldwide. This complete product family delivers precision-engineered performance where mechanical fastening methods fall short, offering solutions for threadlocking, retaining, sealing, and bonding that are trusted by professionals from automotive technicians to aerospace engineers.

Product Line Features

Complete Solution Portfolio

  • Comprehensive range of threadlockers from low to high strength
  • Retaining compounds for cylindrical assemblies and bearing installations
  • Pipe sealants and gasket makers for leak-proof connections
  • Instant adhesives for rapid bonding applications
  • Structural epoxies and acrylics for permanent assemblies
  • Specialty maintenance products including anti-seize and surface treatments

Advanced Anaerobic Technology

  • Revolutionary chemistry that cures in the absence of air between metal parts
  • Remains liquid in the bottle, cures only when confined
  • Single-component formulations require no mixing
  • 100% active with no solvents or shrinkage
  • Room temperature cure without special equipment
  • Fills microscopic gaps automatically for perfect sealing

Professional-Grade Performance

  • OEM-approved formulations used by leading manufacturers
  • Meets or exceeds aerospace, automotive, and military specifications
  • Consistent, repeatable results across applications
  • Temperature resistance from -65°F to 400°F (some formulations to 1800°F)
  • Superior chemical and vibration resistance
  • Long-term durability in harsh environments

Versatile Strength Options

  • Low-strength removable threadlockers for adjustable assemblies
  • Medium-strength products for serviceable fasteners (hand tool removal)
  • High-strength permanent formulations requiring heat for disassembly
  • Customized solutions for specific application requirements

Key Product Categories

Threadlockers (200 Series)

The cornerstone of the Loctite line, these anaerobic adhesives prevent vibration-induced loosening of threaded fasteners while eliminating the need for lock washers, lock nuts, or cotter pins.

Featured Products:

  • 222 – Low strength for small fasteners and adjustable assemblies
  • 242 – Medium strength, the world’s most popular threadlocker, removable with hand tools
  • 243 – Medium strength with enhanced oil tolerance for as-received fasteners
  • 262 – High strength for permanent assemblies on larger fasteners
  • 263 – High strength with superior high-temperature performance
  • 272 – High strength in convenient stick form for clean application
  • 277 – High strength, high viscosity for worn or damaged threads
  • 290 – Penetrating grade that wicks into pre-assembled fasteners

Retaining Compounds (600 Series)

Engineered for cylindrical assemblies, these products fill the microscopic gaps between mating parts, curing to form permanent bonds that prevent fretting corrosion and improve load distribution.

Featured Products:

  • 609 – General purpose high strength for precision bearings and bushings
  • 620 – High temperature, high strength for motor and pump assemblies
  • 638 – High strength with flexibility for shock and vibration resistance
  • 660 – Quick metal assembly for rapid production
  • 680 – High viscosity for maximum gap filling on worn surfaces

Thread Sealants (500 Series)

Replace tape and paste sealants with superior anaerobic formulations that prevent leakage and loosening in pipe and fitting assemblies.

Featured Products:

  • 542 – Medium strength for metal pipes and fittings, allows disassembly
  • 545 – High strength for hydraulic and pneumatic systems
  • 577 – Fast-curing for coarse threads, NSF certified for potable water

Instant Adhesives (400 Series)

Cyanoacrylate adhesives providing rapid bonding of rubber, metal, and plastics in seconds for quick repairs and assembly.

Featured Products:

  • 401 – General purpose fast-curing for universal applications
  • 406 – Low odor, low bloom for visible assemblies

Structural Adhesives

Two-part epoxy and acrylic formulations for permanent bonding of metals, plastics, composites, and dissimilar materials with high shear and peel strength.

Technical Specifications

Anaerobic Formulations:

  • Chemistry: Dimethacrylate ester-based anaerobic adhesives
  • Cure Mechanism: Initiated by metal ions in absence of oxygen
  • Fixture Time: 10 minutes to 6 hours depending on product
  • Full Cure: 24 hours at room temperature (72°F/22°C)
  • Temperature Range: -65°F to 300°F standard (-65°F to 400°F for high-temp variants)
  • Gap Fill: 0.00mm to 0.50mm depending on product viscosity
  • Shelf Life: 12-24 months unopened

Available Viscosities:

  • Low (penetrating): 10-100 cPs
  • Medium (general purpose): 100-1000 cPs
  • High (gap filling): 1000-50,000+ cPs
  • Thixotropic (paste/gel): Non-drip formulations

Packaging Options:

  • Service bottles: 5ml, 10ml, 50ml
  • Standard bottles: 250ml
  • Bulk containers: 1L, 5L, 20L
  • Stick applicators for paste formulations
  • Tube packaging for precise dispensing
