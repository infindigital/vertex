Description

The Vaultex V30 is a professional-grade safety over-spectacle designed to provide high-level eye protection in industrial and construction settings. Engineered with a focus on both safety and comfort, these spectacles feature a sleek, modern wraparound design that offers full coverage for the eyes and surrounding areas while being lightweight enough for extended daily wear.

Key Features
High Impact Resistance: Equipped with durable polycarbonate lenses designed to protect against flying debris and everyday industrial hazards.

UV Protection: The lenses provide 99.9% UV protection, shielding the eyes from harmful ultraviolet rays.

Durable Coating: Features a specialized anti-scratch coating to maintain lens clarity and prolong the lifespan of the eyewear in abrasive environments.

Wraparound Design: The secure, wraparound frame ensures complete coverage and a stable fit during active movement.

Integrated Brow Guard: Includes a built-in brow guard to prevent debris from entering the eye area from above.

Safety Certified: Meets or exceeds EN166 and ANSI Z87.1 industrial safety standards.
