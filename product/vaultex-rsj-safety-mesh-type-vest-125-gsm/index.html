Description

The Vaultex RSJ is a high-visibility safety vest from the UltraPRO premium line, engineered for maximum breathability and safety in professional work environments. This vibrant yellow mesh vest is designed to keep workers cool while ensuring they remain highly visible to others, making it ideal for construction, logistics, and outdoor industrial use.

Key Features
Superior Ventilation: Constructed with a 100% Polyester mesh body with piping to provide excellent airflow for all-day comfort.

High Visibility: Features 4-line retroreflective tape that wraps around the shoulders and waist for 360-degree conspicuity.

Secure Closure: Equipped with a zip closure for a professional and secure fit that remains stable during active work.

Lightweight Durability: Made with 125 GSM fabric, offering a durable yet lightweight solution for high-visibility requirements.
