BOLT MARKERS – Tamper-Proof Indicators

Tamper-Proof Indicator Paste

Available Colors:

  • Neon Yellow – High visibility detection
  • Bright Orange – Easy identification
  • Pure White – Clean professional appearance

Features:

  • Visually detects loosening or movement of fasteners
  • Tamper-evident security seal
  • Works on bolts, nuts, screws, and other threaded components
  • Immediate visual indication if fastener has been disturbed
  • Weather and temperature resistant
  • Provides warranty protection verification
  • Professional forensic-quality indication

Applications:

  • Quality control verification
  • Warranty seal protection
  • Safety-critical fasteners (aerospace, automotive, machinery)
  • Theft prevention
  • Maintenance verification
  • Compliance documentation
  • Critical assembly validation

How It Works: Apply across the fastener head and adjacent surface in a continuous line. If the fastener loosens or is removed, the paint line breaks, providing immediate visual evidence of tampering or movement.
