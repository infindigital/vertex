Description

Honeywell’s High Ankle Safety Shoes combine superior protection with comfort for demanding industrial and workplace environments. Manufactured in India to European safety standards, these professional-grade safety shoes feature genuine leather construction with comprehensive protective features designed to keep workers safe in hazardous conditions.

Key Safety Features

Superior Protection

  • Steel Toe Cap: Provides impact protection against falling objects and compression hazards
  • Steel Mid-Plate: Anti-penetration protection against sharp objects on the ground
  • High Ankle Design: Enhanced ankle support and protection for challenging work environments

Multi-Hazard Resistance

  • Anti-Static properties to prevent static electricity buildup
  • Anti-Acid protection for chemical exposure environments
  • Anti-Alkali resistance for alkaline substance contact
  • Oil resistant sole for slippery work surfaces
  • Slip resistant outsole for improved traction and stability

Comfort & Design

Premium Materials

  • Breathable genuine leather upper for all-day comfort and durability
  • Lightweight construction reduces fatigue during long work shifts
  • Yellow interior lining accent for style and visibility

Advanced Sole Technology

  • Dual density PU injected outsole for optimal shock absorption
  • Padded insole provides cushioning and comfort during extended wear
  • Aggressive tread pattern for superior grip on various surfaces
