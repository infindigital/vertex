Description

The Vaultex CIF (model UB105Plus + WL30) is a premium fall protection system designed for maximum safety and comfort in high-risk environments like construction, demolition, and roofing. This full-body harness is engineered to distribute fall forces evenly across the body, featuring a high-visibility fluorescent green design to ensure workers remain conspicuous in hazardous work zones.

Key Features
Durable Material: Manufactured from 100% High-Tensile Polyester Webbing with reinforced load-bearing stitching for exceptional strength.

Adjustable & Ergonomic: Features fully adjustable shoulder and thigh straps with a specially positioned sit strap for extended comfort during long shifts.

Safety Attachments: Equipped with a single dorsal attachment D-ring for fall arrest and an integrated work-positioning belt with two lateral D-rings.

Enhanced Comfort: Includes a padded waist band with net back support to reduce fatigue and improve wearer compliance.

Shock Absorbing Lanyard: Comes with a 2-meter twin webbing lanyard featuring an integrated shock absorber, an Easy 308 karabiner, and two zinc-plated scaffolding hooks (Easy SH 60).

Globally Certified: Fully complies with international safety standards, including EN 361:2002, EN 358:2018, EN 355:2002, and ANSI Z359.11-2014.
